When you think about 'health,' you might think doctors and medicine. But the Social Determinants of Health is the stuff around that — like where you live, your income, your education, and even your proximity to grocery stores. To me, the Social Determinants of Health essentially means your health is determined by the environment you live in. How much you make, where you live, your education, etc. are all factors that can have a huge impact on your health, long before you even step foot in a hospital.

For example, if you live in a neighborhood with lots of fast food and no grocery stores, you might have a harder time eating healthy. Or if you don't have access to good education, you might not know how to take care of your health properly.

That's where the Capabilities Approach comes in. The Capabilities Approach is a way of thinking about well-being that focuses on what people are actually **able** to do and be, rather than just what they 'technically' have. So for example, if you have a job that pays you enough to afford healthcare, but you live in a neighborhood with no doctors, then technically you have access to healthcare, but in reality, you don't.

So the way I see it, the Capabilities Approach means your capability to be healthy/your well-being is limited by your environment, even if you technically have the resources.

These two concepts are related because the Social Determinants of Health decides a person's capabilities. Meaning, if your environment is bad, then your capabilities are limited from the get go. For example, if you grow up in a poor neighborhood with limited access to education and healthcare, your capabilities to be healthy are already limited, even if you technically have access to healthcare.

## Measures that help understand both concepts

**Social Determinants of Health:**
- Income level
- Education quality
- Proximity to healthcare facilities
- Neighborhood safety

**Capabilities Approach:**
- Ability to afford healthy food
- Ability to access higher education
- Ability to access healthcare when needed
- Ability to live in a safe neighborhood
